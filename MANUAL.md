# The Endless March: Journey to the End

A black-and-white belt-scroller in the spirit of Golden Axe, without the mounts. An outlined figure walks a long white road and fights ten waves along the way. After the tenth, a monolith rises at the end of the road and its door opens onto blinding white light. What that light means is up to you.

This is the complete manual: how to run the game, how it plays, the three classes, every enemy and wave, the art style, and the planned story mode.

## Getting started

1. Download **`The-Endless-March-0.0.1.exe`**.
2. Double-click it. That one file is the whole game: there is nothing to unzip or install.
3. The game opens in its own window. Closing the window closes the game. **F11** toggles fullscreen.

**Needs:** Windows 10 or 11, and Microsoft Edge (built into Windows) or Google Chrome. If neither is found, the game opens in your default browser. Play with the keyboard, the mouse or a gamepad.

The `.exe` isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

The title screen shows the version, **v0.0.1**.

## How a run goes

- **Title → Choose your path.** Pick the Warrior, the Archer or the Rogue.
- **The road.** You start on the plain. The screen locks and **Wave 1** begins: enemies walk in from both edges of the screen or rise out of the ground.
- **Clear the wave → GO →.** Walk right. The camera follows, and locks again where the next wave waits. Between waves you get back 15 HP.
- **After waves 3 and 6, night falls.** Rest, fight off thieves, and wake with 25 HP back.
- **Wave 10** is fought in the void at the end of the road, with a wider view.
- **Then the door.** A monolith rises. Walk to it.

You have three lives. When the last one is gone, the run is over and starts again from Wave 1, with the same class. There are no checkpoints and no permanent upgrades: you get better, not your character.

## The lane rule

The road has depth: you walk up into it and down out of it as well as along it. Wherever you stand, you are in a **lane**, and so is everyone else.

**You only hit what shares your lane.** Every sword slash, arrow and dagger cut keeps to the lane you are standing in. Step up or down to line up with a target, and step out of a lane to dodge what is coming down it.

There is one exception: the Warrior's **sword wave**. The third slash of the Warrior's combo releases a crescent of force that rolls forward along the whole road and strikes anything on the ground it passes, in every lane. Magic also reaches the whole screen, but that is a spell, not a weapon. The Warrior's multi-lane powerup and the Archer's arrow shower also reach every lane while they last.

Enemies follow the same idea: they line up with your lane before they attack, and the dotted lines some of them draw on the floor show you the lane their attack will travel down.

## The three paths

### Warrior · 100 HP

The enormous sword. Slow, heavy, sweeping.

- **Combo:** 15 → 18 → 28 damage. The third slash knocks down and releases the **sword wave** (12 damage, every lane, about 400 units).
- **Running attack:** a shoulder-first lunge that knocks down.
- **In the air:** a downward slash.
- **Back attack:** a full spin of the sword.
- **Class power (capsule):** for 12 seconds every sword hit lands in every lane.

### Archer · 90 HP

Arrows down the lane you stand in.

- **Combo:** arrow (12), arrow (12), **fire arrow** (20). The fire arrow pierces everything in its lane, knocks down, and sets its targets burning: 4 damage every half second for about two seconds.
- **Running attack:** skid and loose one heavy piercing shot (16, knockdown).
- **In the air:** an arrow angled down at the floor.
- **Back attack:** a sweeping bow kick.
- The bow tilts up at an enemy in the air, but only if it is in your lane. Arrows stick in the ground and in ledges where they land, and glance off raised shields.
- **Class power (capsule):** for 12 seconds every shot also brings a shower of six arrows down across every lane ahead. Fire arrows bring down fire.

### Rogue · 85 HP

Twin daggers. Every strike cuts twice.

- **Combo:** twin strike (8 + 8), twin strike (8 + 8), cross cut (18, knockdown).
- **Dash** (Shift / C): a quick dash in any of eight directions, including up and down the lanes. You can't be hit while it lasts. Short cooldown, one dash per jump.
- **Dash twin strike:** attack out of a dash (or while running) to lunge straight through the enemy line, cutting twice, untouchable.
- **In the air:** a dagger dive. **Back attack:** a spin of both blades.
- **Class power (capsule):** wings, and 6 seconds of flight to spend within 25 seconds. Hold jump in the air to rise well above the height of a jump and steer freely.

All three classes can grab and throw, back attack, cast magic and rage.

## Combat

- **Windup, strike, recovery.** Every attack, yours and theirs, has a windup, a moment where it can hit, and a recovery. Enemies shake and a ring closes in on them before they strike. Their recovery is your opening.
- **Combos.** Keep pressing attack to chain three hits. The third knocks lighter enemies to the floor, where they can't be hit until they stand up.
- **Running attack.** Double-tap a direction (or hold run) and attack. Each class has its own.
- **Jump attack.** Attack in the air: it hits what is below you and knocks down. One per jump.
- **Back attack.** Hits both sides at once. For when you are surrounded.
- **Grab, knee, throw.** Attack an enemy right in front of you, at your own height, to grab it. Keep attacking to knee it twice, then throw it over your shoulder. A thrown body knocks down anyone it lands on in its lane. Big enemies can't be grabbed.
- **Parry.** A sword swing that is active when it touches a thrown axe cuts the axe out of the air.
- **Knockdowns.** Heavy hits, or three hits in quick succession, put you on the floor too. While you lie there and stand up, nothing can hurt you.

## Ledges, blocks, powerups and rage

- **Ledges.** Fourteen raised slabs on legs stand along the road; three of them move (two slide back and forth, one rises and sinks). Jump up through one from below and land on top, and a moving one carries you. Walkers climb up after you if you stay close; flyers don't need to. A ledge fades out while you stand behind it, so you never lose sight of yourself.
- **Power blocks.** Thirteen floating blocks marked with a star. Jump into one from below, strike it, or shoot it, and it pops out a powerup. An empty block stays behind and you can stand on it. They refill every run.
- **Heart:** gives back 40 HP.
- **Force field** (a hexagon): a dashed bubble that takes the next three hits for you, for up to 16 seconds.
- **Rage burst** (a spiked star): fills the rage meter at once.
- **Class capsule:** a pill marked with your class's sign; each class gets its own power (see [The three paths](#the-three-paths)).
- **Drops.** Enemies drop a heart, a force field or a capsule now and then (8% of kills); Brutes and the Elite always drop one.
- **Rage.** The meter fills as you land hits (2.5 each), make kills (8) and take damage. When it's full, the HUD flashes **PRESS R**. For 8 seconds you do 1.5× damage (arrows and the sword wave too) with heavier knockback, move 15% faster, take 30% less damage, and nothing staggers you or knocks you down. An outlined spiky aura shows it.

## Magic, thieves and food

- **Thieves.** Small, harmless, carrying a sack. They wander through some waves and come for you at night. Each hit knocks something loose: usually a **magic pot**, sometimes **bread**. After two hits they flee.
- **Pots and bread.** Walk over them to pick them up. You can carry six pots. Bread gives back 30 HP. Unclaimed pickups vanish after a while.
- **Casting.** V spends **every** pot you carry. The world holds still, the screen flashes into negative, and black spikes erupt from the floor under every enemy on screen: 10 damage plus 9 per pot, and everyone is knocked down. More pots, bigger spell.

## Nights

After waves 3 and 6 the march stops for the night. The whole world inverts: white figures in a black land. Thieves creep in for your sack. Take back what they carry. At dawn you are back to white, with 25 HP restored, and the road continues.

## Health, lives and falling

- HP is shown as ten blocks. You recover 15 HP between waves, 25 after a night, 30 from bread and 40 from a heart.
- You have **three lives**. When your HP runs out you fall, and your weapon is left in the ground. If you have lives left, you stand back up where you fell with full HP, and the shock of it knocks nearby enemies off their feet.
- When the last life is gone, the run ends: **FALLEN**. Press any key to begin again from Wave 1 with the same class.

## Enemies

Enemies take turns. Only a few may wind up an attack at the same time (more in later waves); the rest circle you at a distance, waiting for an opening. Every dangerous attack has a telegraph: the enemy shakes and a ring collapses onto it. Then comes the attack, then a recovery you can punish.

| Enemy | HP | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Seeker | 30 | Walks up and claws. The basic pressure. |
| Chaser | 20 | Fast. Circles you, lines up, crouches, and lunges down its lane (dotted line). Step out of the lane. |
| Brute | 100 | Slow, can't be staggered. Raises its fists: the dashed box on the floor is where the slam lands. It knocks you down. Punish the recovery. |
| Axe thrower | 25 | Keeps its distance, lines up with your lane (dotted floor line), and throws a spinning axe. Change lanes, or cut the axe down. |
| Flyer | 30 | Hovers overhead. Marks its landing spot on the floor, then dives. Stuck on the ground for a moment after. |
| Splitter | 40 | When it dies, it splits along its crack into two small, fast Splitlings. |
| Shielder | 60 | Blocks everything from the front, arrows and sword waves included. Turns slowly. Walk around it, hit it from above, or punish its bash. |
| Assassin | 35 | Fades almost to nothing and drifts behind you, then reappears and strikes. Its faint trail gives it away. |
| Elite | 170 | Takes reduced damage. An axe volley fanned across the floor, a dashing slash down its lane, a slam on both sides. Faster below half health. |
| Thief | – | Harmless. Carries pots and bread. |

Walkers climb onto ledges after you. An enemy's health bar shows above it for a moment after it is hit; the Brute and the Elite get a boss bar at the bottom of the screen.

## The door

When Wave 10 falls, the fighting stops. At the far end of the road a monolith rises out of the horizon, a door at its foot. As you walk toward it, the door opens, and light pours out over everything: the stone, the road, you. Walk into it.

The game doesn't say what is behind the door.

## Tips

- Watch the floor. Dashed boxes, dotted lines and target marks show where an attack will land before it does.
- Kill axe throwers early, or stay out of their lane.
- Don't swing at a Brute's windup. Step aside, then hit it while it recovers.
- Save pots for Wave 9 and the Elites. Six pots at once is far stronger than six single casts.
- Open the power blocks as you pass them, and save rage for a crowd or an Elite.
- Warrior: the sword wave is how you reach an enemy in another lane. Finish your combos.
- Archer: range is safety. Line up, shoot, and step out of the lane when something winds up.
- Rogue: the dash is a dodge first. Dash through, then attack out of it.

## The screen

The HUD sits small in the top-left corner:

- **WAVE** and your class.
- **HP** as ten blocks.
- **MAGIC** as six flasks.
- **RAGE** as a bar; it flashes PRESS R when full, and drains with stripes while you rage.
- **LIVES** as little figures.
- **ENEMIES** left in the wave.
- Timers for the force field (with its hits left), your class power and the Rogue's flight fuel.

Top right counts your **chain of hits** until you're hurt or stop hitting for two seconds. Chevrons at the screen edges point to enemies out of view, and **GO →** tells you when to walk on.

## Art style

Ink and paper: everything is drawn live in code, every frame, from a few lines, circles and polygons.

- **Two colours.** Black and white. Grey only ever appears as something fading: a dimmed path on the class screen, a ghost of a dash, light washing over the world.
- **Everything is outlined.** Anything with a body (people, creatures, weapons, pickups, buildings, rocks, ledges and blocks) is a white shape with a black outline.
- **Solid black is for the floor.** Shadows, dust, sparks and the ink that enemies rise out of stay solid black.
- **Hits flip the ink.** For the frame something is hit, its body fills solid black.
- **Readability first.** Every dangerous attack has a shape you can learn.
- **Nothing is textured.** No gradients on bodies, no hatching, no pixel art.

**The road** has four stretches, each with its own horizon: the plain (waves 1–3: low hills, bare trees), the ruins (4–6: broken columns, arches, fallen blocks), the fortress (7–9: walls, towers, rows of spears) and the void (10: almost nothing, and the view pulls back).

**Night** doesn't add darkness: it swaps the two colours. **Magic** flashes the screen into negative as the spikes land. **The light** behind the door is the only thing brighter than the paper: it washes the outlines themselves away.

## Story mode (planned)

Story mode is **not in the game yet**. The current game, ten waves and the door, stays as it is, as arcade mode.

The same road, told in chapters, with almost no words. It adds places, not power: stones carved with lines by earlier walkers, relics of those who fell, camps where a chapter is saved, and full-screen ink panels between chapters.

- **Premise.** There is a road across the white. People say there is a door at the end of it that opens onto whatever you wanted most. Many have set out. Nobody has come back to say whether it's true. You are one more walker on the road.
- **Three reasons to march.** The Warrior finds broken blades planted along the road. The Archer follows cold campfires, always a day ahead. The Rogue has walked this road before, and turned back; the thieves seem to know them.
- **Chapters.** Prologue: The First Step (the plain) · I: What Was Left (the ruins) · II: The Wall (the fortress, and the Gatekeeper) · III: The White (the void, and the Echo, a figure that fights like your own class) · Epilogue: The Door.
- **How it plays.** Chapters save at camps. Same combat. No experience, gear or skill trees. One run per class, each short enough for an evening.
- **What it will never say:** what the walkers wanted, what is behind the door, or whether getting it is good.
